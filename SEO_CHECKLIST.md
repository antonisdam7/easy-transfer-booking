# SEO Execution Checklist

## Search Console and Webmaster Setup

- Verify `https://habibitransferscrete.com` in Google Search Console
- Submit `https://habibitransferscrete.com/sitemap.xml`
- Verify domain in Bing Webmaster Tools
- Submit sitemap in Bing Webmaster Tools

## Google Business Profile

- Keep business name, phone, and email identical to website
- Add service areas in Crete
- Add transfer-focused service descriptions
- Request customer reviews weekly

## Citation and Link Building (First 30 Days)

- Add business profile to 10-20 relevant travel and local directories
- Publish 2 partner links from hotels/villas/tour operators
- Publish 2 supporting blog/guide articles monthly and interlink to:
  - `/crete-transfers`
  - `/heraklion-airport-transfer`
  - `/chania-airport-transfer`
  - `/private-taxi-crete`

## Monthly SEO Iteration

- Review Search Console query performance weekly
- Optimize pages ranking positions 5-20 by:
  - improving title and meta description CTR
  - expanding FAQ sections
  - adding internal links from other pages
  - improving image sizes and page speed
